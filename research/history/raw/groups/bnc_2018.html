Brand NEW Congress candidates
Here are just a few of the exceptional leaders you nominated who have launched congressional campaigns! They are public servants, local leaders, and national figures. All Brand New Congress Candidates have pledged to take no corporate PAC money and no corporate lobbyist money. Meet the candidates and join their campaigns! The race for a corruption-free Congress in 2018 has begun.

James Thompson

Candidate for House of Representatives - KS-04

James Thompson is a veteran, a civil rights attorney, and the Democratic nominee for Congress in Kansas’s 4th congressional district. After nearly winning last year’s special election to replace Mike Pompeo, he immediately announced his run for 2018. He defeated challenger Laura Lombard in the Democratic Primary with 19,844 votes.

James will face first-term incumbent Ron Estes (R) in November’s General Election.

Learn James’s story and how to help him win the general election 

Zak Ringelstein

Candidate for United States Senate - ME

Zak Ringelstein is a public school teacher, entrepreneur, education activist, and Democratic nominee for US Senate in Maine. Zak is not taking a single penny from corporations, PACs, lobbyists, or special interests of any kind because he believes it is time for the working class to write the laws in Washington.

Zak received 89,841 votes in the Democratic primary, and will face incumbent Angus King (I) in November.

Learn Zak’s story and how to help him win the general election 

Dr. Rob Davidson

Candidate for House of Representatives - MI-02

Dr. Davidson is an Emergency Room doctor who believes healthcare is a human right. After confronting his incumbent at a town hall on the issue of Medicare for All, members of the community asked Rob to run for office. He earned 52,221 votes running unopposed in the Democratic Primary, despite spending only 1/10th of incumbent Republican Rep. Bill Huizenga’s primary budget.

Rob will face Rep. Huizenga head on in the November General Election.

Learn Rob’s story and help him win the general election

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

Candidate for House of Representatives - NY-14

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is a former Bernie Sanders organizer, founder of a non-profit, entrepreneur, and the first woman and person of color to win a major party nomination in New York’s 14th district. Alexandria defeated the fourth most powerful Democrat in Congress, incumbent Rep. Joe Crowley, in their June primary, with 16,898 votes (a 13.5% margin of victory) despite being outspent 10 to 1.

If she wins in November, she will be the youngest woman ever elected to Congress. Alex faces Anthony Pappas (R) in the General Election.

Learn Alex’s story and help her win the general election.

Marc Whitmire

Candidate for House of Representatives - TN-02

Marc is a veteran and small business owner running for Congress. He is running as an Independent candidate in the November general election.

Learn Marc’s story and help him win the general election

Dr. Danielle Mitchell

Candidate for House of Representatives - TN-03

Dr. Mitchell is a family physician who runs a community clinic to help working class families have more affordable access to healthcare. She earned 30,927 votes running unopposed in the Democratic Primary. She faces incumbent Republican Rep. Chuck Fleischmann in the November General Election.

Learn Danielle’s story and how to help her win the general election

Lorie Burch

Candidate for House of Representatives - TX-03

Lorie Burch is an attorney who advocates for women, minorities, people with disabilities, and the LGBTQIA community, who earned the Democratic nomination for the Texas 3rd Congressional District. Lorie won the Democratic primary runoff with more than 73% of the vote, for a combined total of 24,896 votes in the primary and the runoff, and became the first woman to win the nomination of a major party in TX-03.

She faces Republican Van Taylor in the race to replace the retiring GOP incumbent this November.

Learn Lorie’s story and how to help her win the general election

Vanessa Adia

Candidate for House of Representatives - TX-12

Vanessa Adia is a teacher and the Democratic nominee for the Texas 12th Congressional District after she earned 21,018 votes running unopposed in the Democratic Primary. Vanessa spent only $55,241, which was 4.5% of the more than $1.2M spent by her opponent, incumbent Republican Rep. Kay Granger.

Learn Vanessa’s  story and how to help her win the general election

Adrienne Bell

Candidate for House of Representatives - TX-14

Adrienne Bell is a teacher, a former Obama organizer, and the first woman of color to be nominated by a major party in the Texas 14th Congressional District. Adrienne secured the Democratic nomination with a decisive victory in the March primary, where she earned 80% of the vote, receiving 19,600 votes.

She faces Tea Party Republican incumbent Randy Weber in the November General Election.

Learn Adrienne’s story and how to help her win the general election

Linsey Fagan

Candidate for House of Representatives - TX-26

Linsey Fagan is a proud single mom, a tech entrepreneur, and the first woman to secure a major party nomination for Texas’s 26th Congressional District. Linsey earned 13,895 votes in the Democratic primary to become the nominee.

She will face off with incumbent Rep. Michael Burgess, a Tea Party Republican and staunch Trump ally, in the November General Election.

Learn Linsey’s story and how to help her win the general election

Sarah Smith

Candidate for House of Representatives - WA-09

Sarah Smith is a working class woman, activist, and organizer running on a bold anti-war platform to represent Washington’s 9th Congressional District. With a team of fiercely dedicated volunteers Sarah earned her place in November’s General Election with 39,409 votes on a budget of just $1.05 per vote, all while continuing to work a full time job.

She faces 22-year incumbent Rep. Adam Smith in the November General Election.

Learn Sarah’s story and how to help her win the general election
